# Business-Data-Anaytics
Projects from first module of LSE Accelerator.

Developed business-focused analytical approaches, worked with datasets in Excel and SQL, and used visualisation to communicate patterns, trends and recommendations.

Marketing Customer & Campaign Analysis:
•	Analysed a supermarket marketing dataset to understand customer characteristics, purchase behaviour and the relationship between advertising channels and customer response.
•	Cleaned inconsistent dates and converted income and other fields to usable numeric formats for accurate aggregation and comparison.
•	Used SQL to query, aggregate and validate data before developing an interactive Tableau dashboard with KPIs, filters and customer/marketing segments.
•	Identified customer and product trends and translated results into recommendations for marketing activity and business decision-making.

Chinook Database Analysis:
•	Wrote SQL queries to retrieve customer first name, last name and country; calculate invoice totals for December 2009; and return composer and track runtime information.
•	Applied SQL best practices including selecting only required columns, clear naming and structured queries to support accurate and readable analysis.
